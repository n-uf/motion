Kit toolbar cases

Cases from moving the dossier kits onto the packages, on the real code: each window lays its toolbar groups out with @n-uf/placement/toolbar, offers drop targets from @n-uf/placement/targets, decides with @n-uf/motion/drag's intent and moves the real groups with @n-uf/motion/layout. Drag a group by its handle (the three dots), or Tab to a handle and use the arrow keys. The slot is the group's own outline where it would land. Sample content only.
