Kit toolbar cases

Three cases from moving the dossier kits onto the packages, on the real code: each window lays its toolbar groups out with @n-uf/placement/toolbar, offers drop targets from @n-uf/placement/targets, decides with @n-uf/motion/drag's intent and moves the real groups with @n-uf/motion/layout. Drag a group by its handle (the three dots), or Tab to a handle and use the arrow keys. The slot is the group's own outline where it would land. Sample content only.

6 · Constraints

The kit's rule is placement's allows(group, place): hostTargets and floatTarget leave a forbidden place out, so createDragIntent never sees it. Redact stays in the top bar (no other bar, no float); Share can't float. Over a forbidden spot there is no target and no slot, the group follows the pointer, and a release glides home. Navigate, Zoom and Annotate go anywhere.

7 · Keyboard moves

Tab to a handle. → ↓ step to the next place and ← ↑ to the previous one: the slot shows it and the group glides there. Enter or Space drops; Esc glides home. Shift+F10 (or a right-click on the handle) opens the menu, whose Move to › lists the same places in the same order. Both read placement's keyboardStops: title → top → left → right → bottom, zones start → centre → end, each insertion point in order, the group's own place among them (where the arrows start).

  1. Focus a handle to see its places.

10 · Scaled page

The desk is scaled with transform: scale(s). The intent works in page space, so the kit converts the pointer first: page = (pointer − origin) ÷ s. The orange cross is the point the intent reads; with the conversion it sits under the pointer and the slot lands there at every scale. Turn the conversion off to see the bug: at 0.7 the intent reads a point away from the pointer.
