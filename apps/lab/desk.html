Desk constructions

Nine ways to build a desk from a toolbar, five tool panels (Files, Outline, Notes, Inspector, History) and open documents. Pick one with the Construction switch; change the stage's size to see how it gives way when width or height runs out. Every item glides by the motion rules: a move settles, a size change is revealed rather than stretched, a panel that folds away fades with a uniform scale. Drag a panel by its title bar (or its tab), and in the tiling tree and the strip a document too: each construction offers its own drop targets, and the drop indicator shows only once you aim (slow down or hold still), never while you fly over targets. From the keyboard: focus a title bar, Space picks it up, the arrow keys step through the targets, Enter drops, Escape cancels. Every frame is sampled and checked below.
