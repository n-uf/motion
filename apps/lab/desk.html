Desk placement

A desk: panel columns on the left and right, a band along the top, a work area for documents, and things pinned over the view. Drag the toolbar by its grip (⠿) into the band (left or right end), a column or the view; double-click the grip to collapse or expand it, right-click it for Horizontal / Vertical; drag a panel by its title bar into a column (at an index) or the view; fold a panel with ▾. A toolbar's size jumps to its new place's content size and only its position settles. Documents tile (Hyprland's master or dwindle) while every tile stays at the minimum size, else they go to the first free spot, else cascade. Every move is sampled per frame and checked below.
