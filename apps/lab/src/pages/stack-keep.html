The stored form (what Reload reads) 






  
Try this

  
    
    
  1. Move things about: drag Pages below Annotations, float Findings, fold a panel by double-clicking its header, tick Kept. Press Reload: the page forgets all of it, then rebuilds from the stored form, and every item is back in its place and form. Reload the browser tab: the same.
    2. 
    
  2. Tick Freeze: the grips disappear and nothing moves, double-clicking a header does nothing. Hold Alt: the grips are back, and a drag or a fold works, on a folded panel's header too. Let go of Alt mid-drag: the drag finishes.
    3. 
    
  3. Change an item's count (try 100 and 12345: the badge says 99+) or its phase: no other item moves, not by half a pixel.
    4. 
    
  4. Fold a panel by hand: the items after it do move (it changed size along the stack), which is what the check for a count or a phase rules out.
