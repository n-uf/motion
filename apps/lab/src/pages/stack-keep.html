The stored form (what a rebuild or a reload reads) 






  
Try this

  
    
    
  1. Move things about: drag Pages below Annotations, float Findings, fold a panel by double-clicking its header, tick Kept. Press Rebuild from the stored form: the page forgets all of it, then rebuilds from the stored form, and every item is back in its place and form. Reload the browser tab: the same.
    2. 
    
  2. Fold both panels by hand: the column becomes a rail of icons. Click an icon (or a folded header's ▸) to open that panel; a click never drags, and a drag never opens.
    3. 
    
  3. Tick Freeze: the grips disappear and nothing moves, double-clicking a header or clicking an icon does nothing. Hold Alt: the grips are back, and a drag, a fold or a click works, on a folded panel's header too. Let go of Alt mid-drag: the drag finishes.
    4. 
    
  4. Change an item's count (try 100 and 12345: the badge says 99+) or its phase: no other item moves, not by half a pixel.
    5. 
    
  5. Fold a panel by hand: the items after it do move (it changed size along the stack), which is what the check for a count or a phase rules out. Tick Size bar groups from their content and raise Views' count to 12345: now the badge's digits change the group's size and the groups after it move, which the check catches.
