Try this

  1. Drag Zoom to the bottom bar, then Notes into the right dock with the kits' own drag. The group lifts and lands on a spring; the panel ghosts, shows a line wherever the pointer is, and jumps.
  2. Switch to ./dock. Drag History into the left dock and hold: room opens after a beat, and the least recently used section folds to make way.
  3. Drag a panel over the document: it tears off as a palette. Drag the palette onto a dock or a rail icon to dock it back.
  4. Fling a group or a panel across the bars and docks without stopping: nothing shows until you slow down.
