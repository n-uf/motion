The column and the bar are two independent examples of the same six items: each has its own ages, kept and open state, so a click on the left never changes the bar on the right. The bar is a toolbar edge: a panel there has no open form (it starts at its header), and the bar is at most 40 px tall.

Try this

  1. Drag Length down. The panel used longest ago folds first (Pages): to its header, then a rail icon, then into the » menu; then the next. Drag it up: they come back. Spare length goes to the open panels.
  2. Click a panel's title in the column (a use) and shorten again: the column's order changes and the bar's does not. Tick Kept for a panel: it goes last in that stack.
  3. Untick every panel's open: the column has nothing open to set its width, so it becomes a rail of about 30 px. Untick the panels' shown too, leaving only groups: still a rail, with icons.
  4. Open or fold one panel: no other panel's open state changes.
  5. Drag the Column width limit down: the groups beside an open panel step from a row of words to stacked words, then icons, as the column narrows. The bar along the top does the same along its length.
