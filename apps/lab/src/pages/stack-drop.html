Try this

  1. Drag a panel or a group by its grip into any gap of the column (above, between, below the items) or of the bar. A dashed rectangle shows the exact place it gets, and a line marks where its leading edge lands.
  2. Hold still over a gap for a moment: the others move aside to the places they will have, and the dashed rectangle sits in the gap that opens. Let go: the item lands there, on the same rect.
  3. Drag a group (Views, Tools, Zoom) into the column of panels: it takes the largest form that fits. Drag a panel into a bar of groups: the bar grows to the panel's height.
  4. Drag everything but the groups out of the column: it is a rail of about 30 px, and the groups are icons. Drag a panel in: the column opens to its width.
  5. Shorten the column or the bar with the sliders and drag again: the older items give way before the one you drop, which counts as just used.
