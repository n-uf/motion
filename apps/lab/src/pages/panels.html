Differences from the ./dock constructions

Edge docks (docks)

  • Width: a dock is a fixed 246 px; a preset column takes its panels' natural width within their min and max, and can be resized.
  • Gaps: ./dock leaves 6 px between sections and around the docks; a preset lays columns and sections edge to edge.
  • Rails: ./dock always shows a 34 px rail of icons on both sides; a preset shows a rail (36 px, an option) only while a column is folded to it.
  • Width give-way: ./dock folds a whole dock to its rail when the work area gets too narrow (the right one first, or the one not opened last); a preset first narrows columns toward their min, then folds columns by the give-way policy (oldest by use, kept last).
  • Height give-way: ./dock folds the least recently used sections to 22 px headers; a preset folds sections by the give-way policy (kept last, never the last open one) to 28 px bars (an option), and only for panels with a declared height.
  • Last section: ./dock gives it at least its natural height; a preset gives it the rest, never under its min (it may go under its natural height).
  • Not in the preset: the toolbar itself and the documents' tiles (the app wires and draws them). The preset keeps the toolbar's room clear when the app asks (reserve, the Toolbar room sliders).
  • Notes: dock-folded and sections-folded become column-folded and section-folded, one per item. Panel ids are strings, not panel:<n> numbers.

Edge docks, full-width bottom (docksFull)

  • All of the above; the bottom band spans the whole width and the columns stand on it.

Edge docks + tear-off (dockTear)

  • All of the above.
  • Float size: a torn-off panel is 200 px wide and its natural height in ./dock; in a preset it has its def's float size, and can be resized.
  • Where it floats: ./dock cascades torn-off panels from the documents' top right and snaps a drop to the documents' edges within 18 px; a preset lands a drop where it was let go, without snapping, and the clamp keeps it in the view.
  • Floating area: ./dock offers floating only over the documents; a preset offers it anywhere on the stage, under the column and rail targets.
  • Too tall: ./dock shades a torn-off panel that doesn't fit the stage's height; a preset shrinks it to the view down to its min, and folds it to a bar only by hand.
  • Start: ./dock starts with nothing torn off; the preset starts with one float.

Navigator + inspector (navigator)

  • Sides: ./dock shows tabs on both sides; the preset tabs the left column and stacks the right one.
  • Width: ./dock's sides are a fixed 240 px; a preset column takes its natural width and can be resized.
  • Narrow: ./dock folds a side to icon tabs (a rail with a segment per panel) when the centre gets under 380 px; a preset narrows, then folds the whole column to its rail by the give-way policy with the pages' 320 px minimum. An icon-tab mode per column is not in this release.
  • Tab strip: ./dock draws rail icons beside its tabs; a preset draws a strip (28 px, an option) at the column's top with equal-width tabs.
  • Tab drop: ./dock inserts a dropped panel at the tab under the pointer; a preset adds it as the last tab, and shows it.

Stored form

Try this

  1. Drag the Width slider down. Columns narrow toward their minimum first, then whole columns fold to the rails, the one used longest ago first. Drag it back up: they return.
  2. Click a panel's title (a use), or the keep button (◇ Keep open), and narrow again: the order changes, and a kept column folds last.
  3. Drag a panel by its title over the pages: it floats. Drag it to a line between columns, into a column, or onto a rail to dock it back.
  4. Scroll the page: Comments moves with it, the pinned Navigator stays put. Narrow the stage: floats stay in view (clamped), or not (Free).
  5. Fold a float with ▾: it shades to its title bar where it is; ▾ again brings it back. On the Options page, pick "Ride on the first open column" and fold a column: its bar sits on top of the side's first open column.
  6. From the keyboard: Tab to a title, Space picks it up, the arrows step through the places, Enter drops, Escape cancels.
  7. On the Options page, switch a band on (top or bottom, reserved or overlay, between the columns or full width): a panel moves into it. Drag panels into the band and out to a side; drag its inner edge to resize it; lower the Height until it folds to a bar, and raise it again. The Toolbar room sliders keep room clear along the pages' edges.
