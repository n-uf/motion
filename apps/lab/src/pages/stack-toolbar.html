Try this

  1. Drag Length down. The groups fold one by one, each to its own handle with a » beside it, in line where the group was (Views, Zoom, Mark, the oldest first). The shared » entry at the end of the bar comes only after the folds.
  2. Tools is the primary: it gives way last. It goes from its full row to its one main tool, and folds only after every other group is folded. Switch it to "used longest ago": it is still the last to fold.
  3. Mark has a stacked form as long as its row. It is never taken: a step goes only to a form that is shorter.
  4. Change the Gap and the Padding: the bar's length counts them, and the groups are drawn at the solver's rectangles.
  5. Drag a group by its grip to another gap, or past the bar's end (the first and last gaps reach 24 px beyond it). Hold: the others move to the places they will have; let go: it lands on the dashed rectangle.
  6. Drag the Findings panel (left, under the bar) into the bar. The bar is 40 px tall at most, so an open panel does not fit: it lands as a header and the bar stays 40 px.
