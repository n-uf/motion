Words

One word for one thing

The site, the READMEs and the lab use these words, and only these, for what they name. The UI says what a person reads (Collapse, Expand); the API keeps the name it already has (fold), so the third column maps one to the other. Spelling is US, as in the API (center).

A window

TermDefinitionAPI name
stageThe window's inner rectangle in px: the area everything is laid out in.DockState.stage, stage
content areaWhat is left of the stage after bars, columns, docks and reserved space: where documents are drawn.DockLayout.content
workspaceThe whole arrangement of docks, panels, toolbars and documents in an app window.DockState
desk layoutOne way of arranging a workspace: which docks exist, what floats, what collapses when. Written down as a dock state.DockState, solve
dock stateThe whole window as one plain-data model: the stage, its edges, the items on them, the floats and the rects kept clear. solve(state) returns every rect, targets(state, id) every drop place.DockState, solve, targets
stored stateA dock state written as plain data and read back over the app's own state after a reload.storeDockState, readDockState
edgeA stack of items along one side of the stage: a bar (runs across) or a column (runs down).DockState.edges, DockEdge
sideOne of the four sides of the stage: top, right, bottom, left.Side
barA horizontal edge holding toolbar groups: top, bottom, or the window's title bar.Orient "row", a DockEdge with side: "top" or "bottom"
columnA vertical edge holding panels and toolbar groups, with its own width.Orient "column", DockEdge.column
title barThe window's own bar: title, window controls, maybe toolbar groups.typically the outermost top DockEdge (ring: 0)
chromeWindow UI that is not content (title bar, toolbars, controls). Floats and drop targets keep clear of the rects the host lists for it.DockState.keepClear
reserved spaceRoom along the content's edges kept clear for something the app draws, such as a toolbar.an edge holding a DockItem.fixed item
hostOnly the app that embeds the library and feeds it input.CaseHost, the host a clock comes from

Items, forms and giving way

TermDefinitionAPI name
item kindThe app's category for an item (panel, group, title). An edge accepts only the kinds it lists.DockItem.kind, DockEdge.accepts
formOne size and shape an item can take: a panel open, as a header, as a rail icon; a toolbar group as a row, as icons.StackForm, DockItem.forms
form ladderAn item's forms, largest first. As room runs out it steps down one rung at a time; when room returns it climbs back in the exact reverse order.forms
Collapse / ExpandThe reversible act of shrinking an item to a smaller form (a header, a rail icon, the overflow menu) and back.foldItem, canFold, foldedBy
headerA collapsed panel: just its title row.formKind: "header"
railA thin strip along a side with one icon per collapsed panel or column. Clicking an icon opens or docks it.rail, DockState.rails
rail modesHow a side's rails show their items: Titles while they fit, or Icons only.RailMode, railModeOf, setRailMode, DockState.railModes
overflow menuThe menu behind the » control, holding the items that no longer fit.menu form, DockEdgeLayout.menu
keep priorityA number per toolbar group: a higher one stays in its full form longer.DockItem.priority
kept openA panel marked "Keep open": it gives way after every unkept one.keepItem(state, id, true), kept
give wayTo yield space: when the stage is too small, items shrink or step to a smaller form in a fixed order. It never writes the user's own collapsed state.contentMin, DockEdge.giveWay
least recently usedThe give-way order: the item (or column) used longest ago yields first. A use is opening, focusing, moving, resizing or expanding.DockItem.used, markUsed

Docking, floating and dropping

TermDefinitionAPI name
dockAn area along a side that holds panels: a side dock, a top dock, a bottom dock.a DockEdge that accepts panels
floatAn item that is not docked: drawn above the content at its own rect.DockState.floats, floatAt
floating panelA panel that floats, at its natural size, with a title bar.DockFloat, DockItem.floatSizes
tear off / dock backDragging a docked panel out so it floats; dragging it back into a dock. liftItem takes the item out of the layout, floatAt makes the float.liftItem, floatAt, dockHome
move toPutting an item at a place: in a dock state, the change that does it; in layout motion, the call that sends an item settling to a new rect.moveTo (@n-uf/docking), LayoutItem.moveTo (@n-uf/motion/layout)
drop targetAny place a dragged item can land.Target, DragTarget
hit areaThe region where the pointer counts as being over a target.Target.hit
slotThe rect the dropped item will occupy: the landing rect.Target.slot
insertion pointThe position between two items where a drop inserts: before all, between, after all, or into an empty zone.gap:<edge>:<zone>:<at>
drop previewEverything drawn while dragging to show the result. The preview equals the result.DragPreview, previewPlan
drop preview modesHow the landing is shown, never where it is: Outline (a dashed outline shows where the item will land; nothing else moves), Placeholder (the outline stays, and once the room is made the neighbors move aside and hold the space), Glide (the neighbors move aside and the item itself glides into the space, with no outline; a landing with no slot keeps its outline), Snap (as Glide with no outline at all: the dragged item is the only preview).PreviewMode ("outline", "placeholder", "glide", "snap"), DragPreview, previewPlan
refusalA place that cannot take the item: one that accepts its kind but has no room now, or a whole edge that doesn't accept the kind (not-accepted). A release over it goes home.DockRefusal, reason
homeThe item's own place when a drag starts. Dropping there changes nothing, and a canceled or refused drag returns there.home, own-place
keyboard stopsThe same drop targets in reading order, for the arrow keys and a "Move to" menu.stops

Landing model and lanes

TermDefinitionAPI name
landing modelWhere a docking drag lands, kept as state: it takes the window's lanes and each pointer sample, and says which lane and spot the pointer holds, where the item lands and whether the room is open.createLandingModel, LandingModel, LandingFrame
laneA strip of the window along one axis (a bar, a rail, a column) where a drop lands on one of its spots.LandingLane, DockLane, lanes
spotOne place a drop in a lane lands: its stretch of the lane and the rect the item takes there.LandingSpot, DockSpot
regionWhere the pointer is for the landing model: in a lane, in open space (a float lands there), over a refused place, or nowhere (past the stage).LandingRegion, LandingFrame.region
gateWhat the pointer must do before the landing model enters a lane: follow it slowly for a stretch, or rest deep inside it. A fast fly-through opens nothing.LANDING_GATES, LandingGates
guideA mark drawn in a lane to show where items can go: a line, a box, or a hatch over a neighbor that gives way.LandingGuide, guideRects
place guidesFaint marks on the free places that take the held item, shown only while no landing shows: Off, Near (the places of the edge the pointer approaches) or All.PlaceGuides, DragPreview.placeGuides, PLACE_GUIDES_DEFAULT

Toolbar groups

TermDefinitionAPI name
toolbar groupA set of related controls that moves, floats and collapses as one unit, led by one grip.a DockItem with GroupFormKind forms
toolbar areaOne of the five places a group can dock: title, top, bottom, left, right.a bar DockEdge, or a compact one at a side
zoneOne third of a toolbar area's length where items align: start, center, end. Only for this.Zone, align
stretch positionWhere a toolbar group lies within the free room between its neighbors in a bar or a toolbar rail (a stretch): the room it keeps on its zone's open side. Free: it lands where the hand holds it, snapped to the stretch's ends and to home. Pitched: it lands on one of the places a pitch apart, the ends included. Either way the order changes only when it passes a neighbor.DockLaneOptions.stretchPositions ("free", "pitched"), DockItem.space, DockSpot.slide
pitchUnder Pitched stretch positions, the step between the places a toolbar group can take in a stretch, in px.DockLaneOptions.pitch, SPOT_PITCH
snap distanceUnder Free stretch positions, how near a stretch's end or home a toolbar group snaps there, in px. A press, a small wobble and a release is home.DockLaneOptions.snapDistance, LandingSlide.snapDistance, SNAP_DISTANCE
gripThe control you press to move a panel or toolbar group. Drag it to move, double-click to collapse or expand, right-click for its menu.createGripPress, StackForm.grip
control ladderA toolbar group's forms made from its controls, so a bar runs short one control at a time and not group by group.controlLadder, ControlLadderSpec
tierOne length per control of a group, longest first (full, shorter, shortest). A group steps through its tiers before it folds.LadderControl.tiers, StackForm.tiers
freeze / frozenThe switch that stops items being moved. Holding Alt lets you move them anyway, while it is held.the app's own freeze flag

Tiling

TermDefinitionAPI name
paneOne item of a tiling: panes divide an area between them, with no overlap and no hole. Not a "tile", which is a rail's icon form../tiling, PaneTarget.pane
order layoutPanes laid out by their order alone: master and stack, columns, a grid. A drop on one inserts the dragged pane at an index.masterStack, columns, grid, orderTargets
split treeHow panes divide an area: each node is a pane, or a split of its rect in two along an axis at a ratio. With no axis it splits along the longer side: dwindle.Split, layoutSplit, splitFromOrder
gutter, handle, dividerThe room between a split's two sides: the gap plus the handle (the extra room a divider takes). The divider is the strip the gutter makes, as a rect.TileOptions.handle, SplitGeometry.divider
floorThe least length a pane keeps along a split; the ratio moves to hold it where both floors fit.SplitOptions.floor
fixed lengthA length a pane keeps along or across a split whatever the ratio.SplitOptions.fixed, SplitOptions.fixedAcross
master, stackA layout of panes in two areas: the first few (the master) on one side, the rest (the stack) on the other.masterStack, Split.master
pane drop regionThe part of a pane under the pointer during a drag: its center (a drop swaps with the pane) or one of its four sides (a drop splits the pane on that side). Not a "zone", which stays a toolbar area's third.PaneRegion, paneRegion, dropOnPane

Fitting and placing

TermDefinitionAPI name
edge cutThe stage cut into bands along its sides, in list order: each band takes its size across from what the earlier ones left, and what remains is the rest.cutEdges, EdgeBand, EdgeCut
stack gapsThe places in a stack where a dragged item could go in, each with the stack it would make, or the reason it does not fit.stackGaps, StackGap
popup positionWhere a popup (a menu, a tooltip, a drop label) goes next to its anchor, flipped, slid or cut to stay inside the bounds.positionPopup, PositionerRule

Drag intent and motion

TermDefinitionAPI name
drag intentThe model that decides, from the pointer's path, when a drop indicator shows, which target it shows, when to make room, and what a release lands on.createDragIntent
liftThe drag threshold: a mouse press becomes a drag after a few px; a touch press after a hold and a few px.lift, touchHold
aimingThe pointer is slow or still enough to mean it. The indicator shows only then.aimSpeed, still, dwell
travelingMoving fast across targets: no indicator shows.aiming: false
hysteresisThe current target keeps the pointer until it is clearly in another one. Geometry, not time.DragTuning.band, bandShare
switch delayWhile aiming, the shown target changes only after the new target has held this long.switchHold
spring-loadingA target that acts when the pointer rests on it during a drag, such as a rail that arms and takes the drop.arm: "dwell", DragTuning.armAfter, DragModifiers.armKey, DragFrame.forced ("armed")
arm keyA held key (⌘ or Ctrl) that sends the drop to the nearest spring-loaded target and arms it, without the pointer reaching it. The frame says the key forced it.DragModifiers.armKey, DragFrame.forced ("armed")
intent boundsThe drawn space of a drag, usually the stage: the intent clamps every point onto it, so a pointer past the window picks what lies against its edge.DragIntentOptions.bounds, DragIntent.setBounds
direct dragThe real item, not a copy, follows the pointer 1:1, drawn exactly where the pointer puts it.holdItem, LayoutItem.hold, HeldDrag.move
settleMotion toward a new target on one fast-then-gentle curve that never passes it. Velocity carries over a retarget.createSettle
layout motionMany items settling to their places on one frame loop: give an item a new rect and it glides there from where it is drawn. An item appears and disappears with a fade.createLayoutMotion, LayoutItem.moveTo, LayoutItem.show, domDrawable
motion valueA value that steps toward its target on a frame loop. Follow it with a listener.motionValue, settleValue, MotionValue.subscribe
frame loopThe loop that runs each frame's work in four phases (input, update, layout, write). It asks for a frame only while it has work.createFrameLoop, Phase
clockWhere the loop reads time and frames from. The host gives one: the browser's, or a fake one for tests.ClockPort, createBrowserClock, createFakeClock
revealDrawing a gliding window without stretching its content: move, draw the frame at the animated size, clip the content.reveal
reduced motionThe user's OS-level preference for less animation. With it on, targets are reached at once.reducedMotion
